# External sources for Meta Data Processor entry

## Research date

2026-09-03.

## Sources located

- Manus Trust Center: https://trust.manus.im/
- Manus Subprocessors: https://trust.manus.im/subprocessors
- Manus Terms: https://manus.im/terms
- Manus help collection on privacy and policies: https://help.manus.im/en/collections/13762620-privacy-and-policies

## Initial finding

The search results indicate that the public Manus Subprocessors and Terms pages are the relevant sources for identifying the service provider and its legal entity. A secondary source mentioned Butterfly Effect PTE. LTD, but that is not sufficient by itself; the official Manus sources must be checked before entering any legal name in Meta.

## Caution

Do not list Meta as the app's data processor merely because Meta supplies the Platform Data. Do not list OpenAI, Claude, or an AI provider unless the current production app actually sends Meta Platform Data to that provider. Use the exact legal/provider name shown in the current official Manus terms or subprocessors page, not an inferred brand name.
